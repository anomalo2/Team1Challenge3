# Known Limitations

- The artwork is intentionally stylized and low-poly, not photorealistic.
- This is an asset-model pack. Complete driving physics, vehicle controllers, traffic AI, siren/flashing-light systems and full animation/controller systems are not included.
- Separate parts and pivots provide geometry/hierarchy only. Useful functionality differs by vehicle.
- Full detailed interiors are not promised. Inspect the included meshes; openable door hierarchy does not imply a fully equipped passenger, cargo or medical interior.
- Simplified LODs omit detail. Detailed bus entry-door openings are provided in LOD0/LOD1; coarse LOD2 retains the closed simplified body rather than an operable door assembly.
- Primitive colliders are practical approximations, not a complete simulation or certified real-world collision model.
- Glass/light appearances are stylized material treatments. No external vehicle texture set is supplied.
- No performance benchmarks or minimum frame-rate guarantee have been established.
- Tested in Unity 6000.6.0f1 / URP 17.6.0. Older versions and Built-in/HDRP are not verified.
- Showcase lighting/shadows can differ with project renderer, quality, color-space and shadow-distance settings.
- The showcase is static. Alternate cameras are disabled by default; there is no runtime camera-navigation system.

There are no known unresolved fleet technical QA defects in the verified project. These scope limits are not promises of unsupported gameplay features.
